筛选说明

The page is capped at 5 papers: frontier papers ≤ 2, historical-impact candidates ≤ 3, and review/knowledge-map papers ≤ 1. Recent papers require research_fit ≥ 40. Historical papers require research relevance and historical_value_score ≥ 42.0. Generic learning, agent, or control terms cannot qualify alone. Dismissal, read status, cooldown, canonical deduplication, and topic diversity apply before selection.

Papers marked “not relevant” are permanently excluded; papers sharing their primary topic are reduced for a short window.
