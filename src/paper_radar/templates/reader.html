筛选说明

The page is capped at {{ max_total }} papers: frontier papers ≤ 2, historical-impact candidates ≤ 3, and review/knowledge-map papers ≤ 1. Recent papers require research_fit ≥ {{ recent_threshold }}. Historical papers require research relevance and historical_value_score ≥ {{ historical_threshold }}. Generic learning, agent, or control terms cannot qualify alone. Dismissal, read status, cooldown, canonical deduplication, and topic diversity apply before selection.

Papers marked “not relevant” are permanently excluded; papers sharing their primary topic are reduced for a short window.
